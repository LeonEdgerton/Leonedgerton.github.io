About Me

I am a Game Development Student at University of Staffordshire specialising in Technical Design and Gameplay Systems. My passion lies in building modular, data-driven frameworks in Unreal Engine 5 that empower rapid balancing and create satisfying player loops.

My Approach to Technical Design

I believe that great gameplay systems should be clean under the hood and seamless for the player. By decoupling game logic from tuning data through structs, Primary Data Assets, and Data Tables, I build flexible architectures that allow for dynamic scaling, live balancing, and rapid iteration without cluttering code.

Professional Foundation

Before stepping into full time game development studies, I gained valuable real world experience in high stakes environments. This background shaped my strong work ethic, disciplined approach to problem solving, and clear communication when collaborating across multidisciplinary teams.
